- **[RACE: Scalable Statistical Estimation of Functional Consistency in LLM Neurons](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24758v1)**

- **[The RAT: A Unified Bayesian Model for RAG Evaluation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24753v1)**

- **[SkillForge: Evolving Verifiable Skills for Reinforcement Learning Agents](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24747v1)**
